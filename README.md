# Overly Honest Resume Generator
Hey! This is a simple web app I built for Stardance. It takes your real thoughts about work (like why you want a job or what distracts you) and generates a funny resume. 
It is a website where you can instantly generate an honest resume that you can email to a company and get a reply from them. Test out your luck to see whether you could land a job with your special skills.

## How to run it
Just open `index.html` in your browser or check out the live demo link below!
  https://sonical015.github.io/Overly-Honest-Resume-Generator/
---

## Features
- Enter custom text input details regarding yourself
- Click buttons to generate and print your resume
- Try the random company application simulator at the bottom
- Check out the preset to fill your info instantly
- Get a reply back from a company to see whether you get hired or not!

- Built with basic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS.
