# Overly Honest Resume Generator
A lightweight, hilarious web app that generates brutally honest corporate resumes from real-world inputs and emails them to companies instantly to help you get a job.
Built for the Hack Club **Stardance** mission!

**Live Demo:** [https://sonical015.github.io/Overly-Honest-Resume-Generator/] 

---

## Features
- **Custom Form:** Takes your real name, desired job title, true motivation and favourite workplace distraction.
- **Honest Logic:** Generates funny corporate resumes and daily duties automatically.
- **Print Support:** Includes a built-in print button formatted to print out the developed resume.
- **Filling Presets:** Includes 2 presets to fill in the information quickly.
- **Submit the Resume:** Includes an option to send your generated resume to a company of your choice and get your results back from them.

---

## Tech Stack 
- **HTML 5:** Layout and input forms
- **CSS3:** Modern card design with google fonts(`Inter`)
- **JavaScript:** Dynamic DOM Manipulation and "window-print()" funtionality as well as the submit resume system
