# Overly Honest Resume Generator
It is a website where you can instantly generate an honest resume that you can email to a company and get a reply from them. Test out your luck to see whether you could land a job with your special skills.

## Website:
**Live Demo:** https://sonical015.github.io/Overly-Honest-Resume-Generator/

---

## Features
- **Customizable Form:** Give your real name, motivation, distraction, and other information thats gets used to generate the resume.
- **Simple Logic:** It generates funny corporate resumes with several sections instantly.
- **Print Support:** It includes a built-in print button formatted to print out the developed resume or save it as a PDF file.
- **Filling Presets:** Includes 2 presets to fill in the information quickly.
- **Submit the Resume:** Includes an option to send your generated resume to a company of your choice and get your results back from them.

---

## Tech Stack 
- **HTML5:** Used to create basic structure and forms.
- **CSS3:** Used to create a clean and professional-looking modern card design along with Google Fonts (`Inter`)
- **JavaScript:** Used for dynamic DOM manipulation and the "print resume" function, as well as generating and submitting resume options.
